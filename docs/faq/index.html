FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything people usually ask before booking a tent house in Bengaluru.

What does H.R. Tent House & Caterers provide?

We rent and set up German hangars, German pagodas, shamianas and canopies; banquet, Chiavari and plastic chairs with covers and bows; long, round and small tables with frills; stage, decoration and lighting; sound; utensils, generators, ACs and coolers; and floor baithak seating.

How much does tent rental cost in Bengaluru?

It depends on the structure and its size, the number of chairs and tables, decor, lighting and sound, the venue location and how many days you need it. Send us your date, venue and guest count on WhatsApp and we'll send a clear quote.

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes. We bring everything to your venue, set it up before the event and take it down afterwards.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Bengaluru and its outskirts, including Hebbal, R.T. Nagar, Yelahanka, Jalahalli, Peenya, Dasarahalli, Vidyaranyapura, Nelamangala, Doddaballapur, Devanahalli and Hoskote. Our base is in Bhuvaneshwari Nagar.

How early should I book?

As early as you can, especially for weekends and the wedding season. Once your date is fixed, message us to block the structures and seating you need.

Can you set up indoors?

Yes. Besides tents, we set up chairs, tables, stages and decor inside banquet halls, offices, conference rooms and school halls.

What is a German hangar (German hanger) tent?

A German hangar is a large aluminium-frame tent with a white waterproof roof and side walls and no poles in the middle. It works like a temporary hall for weddings, receptions, corporate events and exhibitions.

What are your working hours?

We are open all 7 days, 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Setups and take-downs for your event can happen outside these hours. Call or WhatsApp us anytime.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the quote form, WhatsApp us on +91 99020 20244, or call us. Share your event date, venue, type of event and number of guests.
