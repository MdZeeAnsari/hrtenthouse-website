About us

A Bengaluru tent house since 2021

H.R. Tent House & Caterers rents and sets up tents, seating, stages, lighting and sound for weddings, functions and corporate events across Bengaluru.

Our story

From a single shamiana to full event venues

H.R. Tent House & Caterers started in 2021 in Bhuvaneshwari Nagar, Bengaluru. We're a family-run business, and since day one our aim has been simple: turn any open space — a lawn, a school ground, a terrace, a parking lot — into a comfortable, good-looking venue for your celebration.

Today we set up German hangars, German pagodas, shamianas and canopies, along with banquet, Chiavari and plastic chairs, tables with frills, stages, decoration, lighting, sound, utensils, generators and cooling. We work on weddings and nikahs, poojas and house functions, school graduations and annual days, and corporate dinners and exhibitions.
