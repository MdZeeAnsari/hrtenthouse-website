How it works

How we set up your event, in 3 steps

  1. Step 01

    Visit & plan

    Call or WhatsApp us with your date, venue and guest count. We suggest the right structure, seating and layout, and send you a clear quote.

  2. Step 02

    We build it

    Our team arrives ahead of your event to put up the tent, lay out chairs and tables with covers and frills, and set up the stage, lights, sound and cooling.

  3. Step 03

    You celebrate

    You focus on your guests. After the event, we take everything down and clear the venue.
